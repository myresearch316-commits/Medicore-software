Fix Live cross-device sync + strong backend

The foundation: data entered on any device now reaches every device, through a hardened server.

  • DB-optional backend — Node/Express serves the app and stores data in PostgreSQL when available, or a durable file/in-memory store otherwise, so sync never goes dark even if the database expires.
  • Server-authoritative versioning — the server owns a strictly increasing version and always accepts the newest write, so records are never silently dropped (the old bug).
  • Audit trail + feedback tables, automatic database reconnect, and a richer /api/health for diagnostics.
  • Sync status badge (bottom-left) shows each device as "Live sync on" or "Offline — local only"; pull-on-focus + 2.5s polling keep devices current.

UI Interface fixes

  • Settings (gear) and Notifications (bell) buttons now open working dropdown menus (Admin Portal, Sync now, Sign out).
  • Search works everywhere — the home hub filters departments; each department page filters its records live.

A Live admin analytics & payments

  • Time-window revenue — today, yesterday, last 48 hours, last 7 days, last 30 days.
  • Revenue by department (today) and a payment-method split (UPI, Cash, Card, Insurance/claimed).
  • Token cancellations & patients who left without service, and the longest-waiting service.

C Pharmacy module

  • Stock in/out with auto-updating quantities; rack & box location per medicine; expiry tracking.
  • Low-stock & expiry alerts ("recharge the stock"); spent-today, earned-today, stock value.
  • Printable receipts for dispensing; sales flow live into admin revenue.

B Shift & attendance

  • Doctors & nurses clock in / out and apply leave, with automatic timings.
  • Live "currently on duty" list and a full attendance log with worked-duration.
  • Admin sees who logged in/out and when, in real time.

D OPD upgrades — insurance & billing

  • Insurance vs normal patients at registration (insurer + policy); insurance patients flagged in the live queue.
  • Billing tab for all patient/bed charges with paid / unpaid status and printable receipts.
  • Admin shows OPD collected-today, outstanding, and the insurance-vs-normal patient mix.

E Sanitization tracker

  • Raise cleaning tasks after discharge, OT or ICU use; two-step Start → Mark cleaned flow with timestamps.
  • Alerts for areas awaiting cleaning; live status visible to admin.

F Patient Accounts (MRD)

  • Search a patient for a full statement — OPD bills + pharmacy, billed / paid / outstanding.
  • Shows the patient's discharge summary and prints a clean account statement.
  • Overview of every patient sorted by amount outstanding.

G QR feedback

  • Admin generates a QR code; patients & visitors scan it, open a mobile feedback page, and submit feedback or complaints.
  • Responses appear live in the admin portal (admin-only), colour-coded by type.

H Receipts

  • One-click Print receipt in Pharmacy (dispensing) and OPD (patient bills), and printable statements in Patient Accounts.
